What we believe about the marketsand why we built XAU-ELITE-FONDA.

The trading world keeps changing, and so does what moves prices. These are the ideas behind everything we build.

Institutional data for everyone.

Banks and funds have always had research, positioning data and people to ask. Every trader who takes this seriously deserves the same picture.

Knowledge over opinions.

Anyone can have an opinion about the market. We show you the data behind the move, so you build your own view instead of borrowing someone else's.

Context before conviction.

A chart shows you what happened. It doesn't tell you why. Every trade should start with the reason behind the move.

A world that keeps changing.

Rates, elections, wars and new technology. What moves markets today isn't what moved them a few years ago, so the terminal keeps changing with it.

People check the machine.

Our engine sorts thousands of headlines and data points. A human analyst checks what reaches you, because context needs judgement.

You make the decision.

We give you the full picture, explained in plain words, so every decision you make is your own and a better informed one.

What we will never doThese are the lines we don't cross, no matter what the rest of the industry does.

  • ✕Sell you signals or tell you what to trade.

  • ✕Sell you a ChatGPT wrapper running on unverified data and call it analysis.
